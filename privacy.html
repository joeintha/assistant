Privacy policy

This app is used privately by its owner with his own Google account.

  • Data is accessed only on the owner's own device.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • No data is stored on any server for this app.
  • Access can be removed at any time in Google Account settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
